# Hi, I'm Ziqi Liu · 柳子祺

Graduate researcher in integrated circuit engineering at **Beijing Institute of Technology**.

I work at the intersection of **analog CMOS**, **MEMS sensors**, and **integrated sensing systems** — from device physics and electrothermal modeling to low-noise interface circuits and system-level validation.

## Research Focus

- MEMS device modeling and characterization
- Electrothermal co-analysis and device–circuit co-optimization
- Low-noise, high-precision sensor ASIC interfaces
- Biomedical and industrial measurement systems

## Education

- **M.Eng. in Integrated Circuit Engineering**, Beijing Institute of Technology, 2025–2028
- **B.Eng. with Honors in Electronic Information Engineering**, Beijing University of Technology, 2021–2025

## Selected Work

- **IEEE MEMS 2027** (accepted) — A wafer-scale electrical resistance tomography skin for pixel-free high-resolution tactile perception.
- **IEEE IEDM 2026** — Monolithic vertically integrated CMOS–MEMS temperature–humidity–flow sensor with pulsed time-domain multiplexed readout.
- **IEEE NEMS 2026** — Sensitivity-enhanced MEMS–ASIC integrated thermal flow sensor; **Best Student Paper**.
- **IEEE Internet of Things Journal** — Wireless integrated system for COPD monitoring using a high-sensitivity MEMS flow sensor.
